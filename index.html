Metals made simple 🥇

A quick guide to what jewellery is made of.

MetalWhat it isGood to know
Gold (14k / 18k / 22k)Pure gold mixed with other metals. 24k is pure; 18k is 75% gold.Higher karat is softer and richer in colour.
Sterling silver92.5% silver with other metals for strength.Can tarnish; polish with a soft cloth.
PlatinumA dense, naturally white metal.Durable and often used for fine settings.
Gold-plated / vermeilA thin gold layer over another metal. Vermeil uses sterling silver as the base.Avoid water and rubbing to keep the layer longer.
Stainless steelA tough, low-maintenance alloy.Popular for everyday and active wear.

Choosing a necklace length 📏

Length changes how a necklace sits on your neckline. A choker hugs the base of the neck, a princess length rests near the collarbone and longer styles fall lower on the chest.

Try layering two or three lengths with a few inches between each. Keep one piece as the star and let the others stay simple.

Easy styling ideas 🎀

Simple rules that work for most outfits.

Match the neckline

V-necks suit pendants, round necks suit shorter chains, and off-shoulder tops suit statement earrings.

Choose one statement

If your earrings are bold, keep the necklace delicate, and the other way round.

Mix metals with a link

Combine gold and silver by adding a piece that contains both, like a two-tone bracelet.

Think about your hairstyle

Updos show off earrings and necklaces, while loose hair pairs well with studs and small hoops.

Wear what feels like you

Comfort and personality matter more than any rule. Follow what makes you smile.

Care and storage 🧼

Small habits help jewellery last for years.

  • 💄 Put jewellery on after makeup and perfume
  • 💧 Remove before swimming or showering
  • 🧵 Store pieces separately in soft pouches
  • 🧽 Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth
  • 🌡️ Keep away from heat and direct sunlight
  • 🔍 Check clasps and settings occasionally

Stakio FAQ ❓

Short, honest answers.

What is Stakio?

Stakio is an Online Adventures Platform where you can read and learn about jewellery, gemstones and accessories.

Is Stakio a shop?

No. It is an informational space with guides and tips. It does not sell products.

Are birthstones the same everywhere?

Not always. Different countries and traditions list slightly different stones, and many months have alternates.

What if jewellery irritates my skin?

Some people react to certain metals. Try hypoallergenic options, and if irritation continues, please consult a doctor or dermatologist.
